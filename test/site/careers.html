Careers at Packa Corporation

Home » Careers

We've got employees who started on the line in the 1980s and never left, and we've got people who started last year. We pay competitively for the Sedalia area, offer health insurance and a 401(k) with a company match, pay every other Friday by direct deposit, and we try not to make people work weekends unless there's a real crunch.

Current Openings

No Openings at This Time

We don't have any open positions right now. Openings and shifts change throughout the year, so please check back — and if you're interested in working here, we still encourage you to apply. We keep applications on file for 12 months and reach out when something opens up.

How to Apply

The fastest way to apply is in person. Stop by the front office at 1420 East 3rd Street, Monday through Friday between 8:00 AM and 3:30 PM, and ask for an application. Bring a resume if you have one, though it isn't required for production positions.

You can also mail a resume and cover letter to the address below, or email it as a PDF to the address listed. Please put the type of work you're interested in in the subject line.

We do not accept applications through third-party job sites. If you saw this listing posted somewhere else, please apply directly using one of the methods above.

  • 401(k) with company match
  • Paid vacation and holidays
  • Shift differential for 2nd shift positions
  • Steady, year-round work — we are not a seasonal operation
