A Company Built By One Family

Walter Packa started this company in 1958 selling paperboard and twine out of a rented storefront on East 3rd Street. His sons Donald and Gerald took it over in the late 1970s and added corrugated box manufacturing. Today Walter's grandson Steve runs the company, and a good number of our employees have been here twenty years or more.

We're not the biggest packaging company in the country, and we don't try to be. We're the one that answers your email when you write, and the one that will drive a truck out to your dock personally if that's what it takes to get your order there on time.
