Industries We Serve

Home » Industries Served

Most of our customers are within a few hours' drive of Sedalia. We work with businesses of all sizes, from a two-person shop that orders a pallet of boxes twice a year to manufacturers that take a truckload a week. Here's where most of our volume comes from.

Agriculture & Farm Supply

Produce cartons, seed and feed packaging, and shipping boxes for co-ops and farm supply stores throughout central and western Missouri.

Food & Meat Processing

Wax-coated and standard cartons for meat processors, dairy operators and regional food distributors, including several plants in the Sedalia and Warrensburg area.

Furniture & Wood Products

Heavy-duty and telescoping boxes, corner protectors and crating for furniture manufacturers shipping finished goods around the country.

Automotive & Parts Suppliers

Custom die-cut trays and foam inserts for parts suppliers serving assembly plants in the Kansas City and Springfield corridors.

Distribution & E-Commerce

Standard and custom-sized RSC boxes, void fill and shipping supplies for regional distributors and online sellers.

Retail & Point-of-Sale

Printed retail cartons and point-of-purchase displays for grocery and hardware store chains across the four-state area.

Not Sure Where You Fit?

Plenty of the businesses we work with don't fall neatly into one category — a hardware store that also ships mail order, a small manufacturer that also does retail packaging. That's fine. Our sales team can usually tell you over email whether we've got a box or a customer close to what you need.

Tom Bracken handles most of our accounts in Kansas and Oklahoma; email info@packacorp.com and ask for him directly if you're outside the immediate Sedalia area.
