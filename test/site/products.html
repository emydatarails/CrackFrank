Products & Capabilities

Home » Products

Packa Corporation manufactures corrugated boxes and related packaging products on our own equipment at our Sedalia plant. Below is a general overview of what we make. Most jobs are custom — email us your specs and we'll quote it. Our corrugator runs about 1,500 tons of containerboard a quarter, most of it from mills in Missouri and Arkansas.

Corrugated Boxes

Regular slotted containers (RSC) in single wall, double wall and triple wall board, cut and glued on our corrugator and box-making equipment. We stock common sizes and run custom sizes to order.

  • Regular slotted containers (RSC)
  • Die-cut boxes and trays
  • Full overlap and half-slotted containers
  • Telescoping and heavy-duty export boxes
  • Single, double and triple wall board

Printed Cartons & Point-of-Purchase

Our flexo printer handles up to four colors. Most retail and produce customers run one or two colors with their logo and basic product information. We also build point-of-purchase display bins and counter displays for retail accounts.

  • Up to 4-color flexographic printing
  • Retail shelf-ready cartons
  • Produce and agricultural cartons
  • Counter and floor point-of-purchase displays

Custom Packaging & Protective Inserts

We design and cut foam and corrugated inserts, partitions, corner protectors and pads to fit your product and reduce damage claims in transit. Bring us a sample of what you're shipping now and we'll usually have a proposal back within a week.

  • Die-cut foam and corrugated inserts
  • Cell dividers and partitions
  • Corner and edge protectors
  • Slip sheets and pads

Pallets, Crating & Shipping Supplies

Through our sister supplier network we also provide standard and custom wood pallets, export-grade crating, stretch wrap, strapping and void fill so you can source a full shipment through one vendor.

  • GMA standard and custom wood pallets
  • Export crates built to ISPM-15 standards
  • Stretch wrap and pallet strapping
  • Void fill and dunnage
